# Patterns

Recurring dynamics, with dated evidence. Add a line under a pattern each time
the record repeats it. A pattern with one date under it is not yet a pattern —
leave it in **Watching** until it repeats.

---

## P1 — Good stretches don't yet buy credit

A run of good days does not register with her as proof things are fixed while
a bad stretch is still recent. She reaches back past the good week to the last
bad one.

- `2026-08-31` — a good week ends with "nothing has been fixed," anchored to
  the bad stretch ~a month earlier.

---

## P2 — Presence is not legible without the check-in

He substituted *being good and being there* for the explicit check-in. From
her side the effort became invisible: the check-in is what she can see and
count, presence is what she has to infer.

- `late Aug 2026` — he drops the check-in deliberately, in favour of making
  good memories and being present.
- `2026-08-31` — her first-listed grievance is specifically "you didn't check
  in all week," not "you weren't around."

---

## P3 — Grievances arrive attached to an event

The blow-up lands the day before and the morning of something of hers that he
isn't attending. The stated content is about the relationship; the timing is
about the event.

- `2026-08-31` / `2026-09-01` — vet visit he wasn't going to (work). "You're
  going to work" appears in the same breath as "we're growing apart."

---

## Watching (not yet a pattern)

- **Aggression during conflict.** `2026-09-01` — shouting, "go fuck yourself"
  or similar, sustained aggression after he offered and then gave the help she
  asked for. One instance is one instance. Log it plainly each time it
  happens, with what preceded it. If it repeats, it stops being an incident
  and becomes something to name directly with him.
- **Distance-then-door works better than pursuit.** `2026-09-01` — he chose
  a low-pressure text over a call and got a reply. Needs more data.

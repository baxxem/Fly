# Entry template

Copy this block, fill it, append to the bottom of `journal/YYYY-MM.md`.
Leave a field out entirely rather than filling it with a guess.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — <short title>
**Logged:** YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC · <HH:MM local, if known>
**Kind:** incident | check-in | good day | note | follow-up
**Tags:** #morning #check-in #weekend #work #money #pets ...

### What happened
His account, in his framing. Sequence, not summary. Direct quotes in quotes.

### What he did
Every action he took, including the ones he decided against. This is the
column that gets thin in memory and matters most later.

### What she said / did
Observable only. Her words where he remembers them, "he doesn't recall the
wording" where he doesn't.

### Read
Clearly marked as Claude's read, not his. Pattern links to dated entries.
Short. No diagnosis of her.

### Open threads
- [ ] things still unknown or unresolved, `> [!pending]` if he's checking
```
